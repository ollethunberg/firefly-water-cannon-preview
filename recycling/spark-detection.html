See the risk before it glows.

Firefly’s infrared detectors identify hot particles and sparks in a moving material flow, including particles that are not visibly glowing. Detection and extinguishing work together to address ignition sources before they develop into a fire.

Detect

Infrared detection identifies dangerous ignition sources in the material flow.

Respond

The control system activates the configured extinguishing response.

Protect

The arrangement is engineered for the material and process conditions.

Protection within the material flow.

Detection and extinguishing can be applied to conveying and processing arrangements. The required configuration depends on the material, ignition risk and operating conditions.
